All 81 scoring patterns ("fan") from the 1998 Chinese Official Mahjong Scoring Rules, ordered from highest-scoring to lowest-scoring. A few of the rarest patterns are illustrated with an example hand; the rest are described in text. Patterns that depend on how a hand was won (self-drawn, last tile, replacement tile, kong calls, flowers, etc.) aren't detected by the hand builder's scoring tool, since it doesn't track that information.

Shanten counts tiles away from a complete hand. Tile counts assume none of your tiles are visible elsewhere, since discards and other hands aren't tracked here. Named-hand predictions for an in-progress hand assume its partial sets complete as shown.
